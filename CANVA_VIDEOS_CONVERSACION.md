# Canva — Videos de conversación (gratis con prueba)

**Formato:** 9:16 vertical (TikTok)  
**Herramienta:** Canva Magic Media (30 días gratis con Canva Pro)

---

## Cómo empezar

1. Ve a [canva.com](https://www.canva.com)
2. **Crear diseño** → **Video TikTok** (1080 x 1920)
3. Menú lateral → **Apps** → **Magic Media** → pestaña **Video**
4. Pega el prompt y genera

---

## VIDEO 1 — "Hesch scho?" (2 personas en cocina)

### Prompt para Magic Media

```
Create a 15-second vertical video (9:16) of two people having a real conversation at a kitchen table.

Scene 1 (0-5s): Two friends or a couple at breakfast. One person asks "Hesch scho?" (Did you already?) with a curious expression. The other looks confused and says "Hesch scho was?" Natural morning light, kitchen setting, casual clothes. European home. Realistic, authentic, not staged.

Scene 2 (5-10s): First person gestures with hand (pill gesture) and says "Dini! Die wo du jede Morge nimmisch." Second person reacts with "Ach SCHEISSE" - facepalm, stands up quickly. Real reaction, genuine frustration.

Scene 3 (10-15s): First person shows phone with app screen. Second person leans in curious. Text on screen: "MediControl - Link i dr Bio". Warm, friendly resolution. Clean modern aesthetic.
```

### Después de generar

1. Añade **texto** en cada escena con los subtítulos (en Canva → Texto)
2. Ajusta cada texto a 2–3 segundos
3. Usa fuente grande y legible

### Subtítulos a añadir

| Segundo | Texto |
|--------|-------|
| 0 | Hesch scho? |
| 1 | Hesch scho was? |
| 2 | Dini! Die wo du jede Morge nimmisch. |
| 3 | Ach SCHEISSE |
| 4 | Wider vergässe? |
| 5 | I ha e App. Piepst me jede Morge. |
| 6 | MediControl. Link i dr Bio. |

## VIDEO 2 — "Papa und die Zettel" (2 hermanos hablando)

### Prompt para Magic Media

```
Create a 18-second vertical video (9:16) of two siblings or friends having a serious conversation.

Scene 1 (0-6s): Two people sitting on a sofa or at a table. One says worried: "Papa het wider nid gno." (Dad didn't take them again). The other reacts: "Scho?" (Already?). Concerned expressions. Living room, natural lighting.

Scene 2 (6-12s): First person gestures - "I ha im Zettel überall aagmacht" (I put notes everywhere). Second person: "Und?" First person frustrated: "Lige no. Verstoot nid." Shakes head. Realistic family conversation.

Scene 3 (12-18s): First person shows phone with app. "App. Erinnert ihn. Und i gseh uf em Handy ob er's gmacht het." Second person nods. Text: "MediControl - Link i dr Bio". Hopeful, solution found.
```

## VIDEO 3 — "Arztpraxis" (Doctor + paciente)

### Prompt para Magic Media

```
Scene 1 (0-6s): Doctor in white coat at desk asks: "Händ Sie d Medikamente gno?" (Did you take your medication?). Patient looks at phone, says "Moment...". Medical office setting, professional, clean.

Scene 2 (6-12s): Patient shows phone screen to doctor. "Ja. Hüt Morge um 8. Und gester au." Doctor leans in surprised: "Sie händ e... App?" Patient nods: "Ja. Erinnert mich. Und i hake ab." Doctor impressed.
```

## Si Magic Media no genera bien personas

**Alternativa:** Usa plantillas + fotos de stock

1. Canva → Buscar **"TikTok conversation"** o **"TikTok dialogue"**
2. Plantilla con 2–3 escenas
3. **Pexels** (gratis): Buscar "two people talking kitchen", "doctor patient consultation"
4. Sube las fotos y añade los textos como subtítulos
